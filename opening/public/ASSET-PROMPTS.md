# Sclera material

`sclera-v4.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for the
Cajklops opening. It is loaded as the diffuse color texture on the curved eye,
with moving 3D highlights rendered separately. The original generated image is
retained in the Codex generated-images folder.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: diffuse color texture for the visible sclera of a realistic animated 3D human eye. Generate an extremely detailed macro photographic material texture, a wide 2:1 rectangle completely filled with moist ivory-white human sclera. Warm pale ivory in the entire central 60 percent, smoothly turning faintly pink at the far left and right edges. Sparse tiny natural branching reddish capillaries, most visible at the two side edges, very faint and subtle in the middle. Minute organic surface variation and natural translucency color, smooth healthy ocular surface, photographic realism, neutral flat soft illumination. This is an unwrapped seamless-looking material swatch, not a picture of a whole eye. No iris, no pupil, no eye opening or frame, no eyelashes, no skin, no text, no borders. No shiny white glare or baked reflections, no strong shadows, no blue or yellow tint. The texture must fill the entire image edge to edge.
